### 7. **New —** which volume should backups go to? — *partly answered*

One thing worth getting right, because it's the difference between a backup
and the appearance of one: **a separate partition on the same physical drive
isn't protection.** Partitions share the disk. If the drive fails they fail
together, which is precisely the case backups exist for.

So I need the name of a volume that's a genuinely different device. If you can
send me the output of `diskutil list` on the Mac — or just tell me which of
the array's volumes are separate drives — I'll point it there and confirm.

The software checks this itself and warns on every run until the two are
actually on different hardware, so this can't be closed by either of us saying
it's fine; the warning has to stop.

There's also a **password for the backups** now, stored on the Mac. Put a copy
